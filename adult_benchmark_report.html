1 Dataset Overview: OpenML Adult (ID 179)

The Adult dataset (OpenML did=179) originates from the 1994 U.S. Census Bureau database (donated via the UCI Machine Learning Repository by Ronny Kohavi and Barry Becker). The prediction task is binary income classification: predicting whether an individual earns more than $50,000 per year based on demographic and employment attributes.
